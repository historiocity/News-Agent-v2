Far-right gunman kills six in Kraków’s Main Square; three more found at his home

International / NATO ally New subject Independent corroboration Underreported

Independent accounts compared: 4. Negative-check / underreported lead.

A 25-year-old Polish man drove onto Kraków’s Main Market Square around 3 p.m. local time Saturday, fired from a car with a military-style long gun, and killed six people before police shot and wounded him, according to BBC, The Guardian, AP, and Deutsche Welle. Four women and two men died in the square; one confirmed British citizen among the dead; 15–16 injured including two police officers, one critically. Prime Minister Donald Tusk called it an “act of terror,” said three more bodies — likely immediate family — were found at the suspect’s home ~200 km away, and ordered two days of mourning. Interior Minister Marcin Kierwiński said the attacker held “ultra-nationalistic” far-right views and acted alone on present evidence. Local Polish reporting (Onet) names him Michał O. of Ostrowiec Świętokrzyski and notes the father held a multi-weapon permit; neither had prior records.

US channel: Poland is a frontline NATO ally; UK/EU solidarity statements landed within hours; Foreign Office travel advice updated. No US nationals named among the dead at press time.

What to watch: suspect’s hospital status and charging document; whether any network of “supporters” Tusk vowed to detain materialises; UK identification of the British victim.

CENTCOM disables M/V Huda after an Iranian port call — first Gulf disable in a month

International / energy Follow-up Independent corroboration

Independent accounts compared: 3. Trigger: new verifiable kinetic disable of a commercial vessel — continuation of the Hormuz blockade thread from this evening’s issue.

CENTCOM said Saturday that U.S. forces disabled St. Kitts–flagged dry bulker M/V Huda in the Gulf of Oman after the crew ignored repeated warnings and tried to leave regional waters following an Iranian port visit. A U.S. aircraft fired precision munitions at the stern after directing the crew to abandon targeted spaces. Maritime Executive places the ship ~18 nm east of Khor Fakkan via UKMTO and notes it is the first reported disable since the Ocean Molica on 10 October. CENTCOM tallies since mid-July: 147 vessels redirected, five merchant ships disabled, and 10 IRGC-linked oil tankers previously destroyed. Arab News and AP wrap the same primary. Separately, Iranian state media claimed one or two U.S. MQ-9 downs over Qeshm; CENTCOM offered “no comment” and called the area “relatively calm” — treat as unconfirmed.
